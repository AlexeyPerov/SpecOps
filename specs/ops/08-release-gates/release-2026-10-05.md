Real Codex account A/B coexistence and all advertised native account/provider handoff pairs need explicit authorized evidence. Native tool/approval/question/turn-budget/sandbox enforcement and selected OpenCode restoration/tool-management/share/revoke require real native validation. Signed/notarized packaging, current SDK/native redistribution Terms/license/notice clearance are **not run/open**. Cursor's native package has no standalone license; downloading/copying a package does not establish public redistribution permission. Manual installed quit/crash child/grandchild inspection and the immediate unobserved spawn/exit race are **open**. Copied payload/control probes verify asset availability, not installed lifecycle or filesystem isolation. The required platform gate concerns the selected Darwin arm64 combination; other platforms are separately unadvertised and remain open for full roadmap scope.
